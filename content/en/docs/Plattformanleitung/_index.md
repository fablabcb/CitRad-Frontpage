## This is how it works

# Read out data

The data can be read from the SD card. Simply insert it into a standard SD card reader.

# Data upload

You can upload the data from the sensor to our data platform at [upload.citrad.de](https://upload.citrad.de). To upload you need a login, which you can get from us. When uploading, you can mark the measurement location on a map, enter the speed limit there and enter further notes about the location. When uploading, you are welcome to include photos of the location in addition to the measurement data.
